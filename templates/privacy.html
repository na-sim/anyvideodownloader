Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to process download requests:

  • The video URL you submit.
  • Your IP address (for rate limiting and abuse prevention).
  • Basic server logs (timestamps, request paths, status codes).

2. What We Do Not Collect

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or any third-party tracking services. We do not create user accounts or profiles.

3. File Handling

Downloaded files are stored temporarily on the server (approximately 15 minutes) solely to let you save them to your device. Files are automatically and permanently deleted after this period. We do not view, analyse, share, or redistribute downloaded content.

4. Logs

Server access logs may include IP addresses, timestamps, and requested URLs. These logs are used solely for debugging and abuse prevention and are not shared with third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

This service may be hosted behind Cloudflare and on Render. Their respective privacy policies apply to the infrastructure layer.

6. Changes

We may update this policy at any time. The latest version is always available at this page.
